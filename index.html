Background

I’m from Bogotá and live in Barcelona. My work has moved between software engineering, data science, higher education, and learning design.

Eight years in industry taught me to build for real constraints and real users. Academia taught me to ask slower questions. I try to hold both habits together—and to treat technology as a form of service.
