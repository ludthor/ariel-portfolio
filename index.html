About

I'm a Bogotanian living in Barcelona — a researcher, engineer, and educator drawn to the places where data, learning, and people meet. I hold a Ph.D. in Information and Communication Technologies from Universitat Pompeu Fabra, where I studied how the design of learning environments quietly shapes what students experience and what they carry forward.

Before academia, I spent over eight years building software, data architectures, and machine learning systems across Colombia. That hybrid path — industry and research, Latin America and Europe, code and classrooms — informs everything I do. Technology, I've come to believe, is most powerful when it serves the human spirit: as a tool for understanding, for equity, and for connection.

Research

Every course leaves traces — in how it was designed, how students moved through it, and what emerged along the way. My research works with those traces at the institutional scale, bridging Learning Design and Learning Analytics to help universities see the interplay between course design, engagement, and outcomes. I also explore how large language models can help us interpret and orchestrate educational data in ways that were previously out of reach.

Work

Before — and alongside — research, I spent over eight years leading data and ML projects at startups across Colombia. From designing cloud architectures to building prediction models, each role deepened my conviction that the best technical work is the kind that makes complexity disappear for the people who need answers.
